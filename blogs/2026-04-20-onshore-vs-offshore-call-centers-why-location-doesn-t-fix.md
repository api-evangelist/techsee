At its March Open Meeting, the FCC introduced new proposals aimed at limiting the use of overseas call centers and improving customer experience. The discussion reflects a long-standing concern across the industry: that offshore contact centers create friction due to language barriers and communication gaps. The reasoning is easy to understand.
